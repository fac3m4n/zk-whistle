% Abstract (English).

Whistleblowers who expose corruption, fraud, or abuse routinely face
retaliation that ranges from professional destruction to physical danger.
The protection mechanisms available to them are weakest at the two moments
that matter most: while sensitive evidence sits unpublished and vulnerable to
seizure, and when a credible source wishes to be compensated without revealing
who they are. Centralized leak platforms concentrate exactly the trust that a
threatened source cannot afford to grant, while naive on-chain approaches leak
metadata that deanonymizes participants.

This thesis presents \zkwns, a decentralized architecture that addresses these
gaps by combining four cryptographic primitives into a single coherent system.
A \emph{cryptographic dead man's switch} guarantees that withheld evidence is
released automatically if a source goes silent, removing the leverage an
adversary gains by detaining them. \emph{Threshold key management} based on
multi-party computation replaces trusted hardware as the custodian of decryption
keys, so that no single party---and no compromised processor---can unlock a
payload ahead of its conditions. \emph{Zero-knowledge provenance} lets a source
prove, without revealing their identity, that they genuinely hold the
credentials they claim. Finally, \emph{stealth addresses} make financial rewards
unlinkable, severing the payment trail that would otherwise expose a recipient.

A central contribution of this work is a critical re-evaluation of the trust
model. An initial design delegated key custody to a Trusted Execution
Environment; we document why that choice reintroduces a hardware-rooted single
point of failure and we replace it with a threshold network whose security rests
on mathematics rather than on the integrity of a single chip. The complete
system is implemented as a set of audited-style Solidity smart contracts and a
TypeScript client, validated by an automated test suite, and deployed to a
public test network. The result demonstrates that strong, usable source
protection can be assembled from production-ready decentralized components
without trusting any single intermediary.
